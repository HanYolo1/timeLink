## A new branch. Shared roots.

**timeLink is a fork of [Hookshot](https://github.com/NotRexed/Hookshot), created by NotRexed.**
This fork is continuing that work in timeLink, with its own development, fixes and community.
The original project and its contributors made this possible; their history and acknowledgements remain part of timeLink.

timeLink turns your own supported copy of the game into a native Windows or Linux program.
That process is called **static recompilation**. The build happens on your PC, and your game files stay with you.

## Built by its contributors

We're here for the code, the details and the people who play. Help us make timeLink easier to use:
share a useful bug report, improve a guide, test a change or contribute a fix.
No bean expertise required.
